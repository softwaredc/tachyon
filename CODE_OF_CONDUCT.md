# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of experience level, education, socio-economic status,
personal appearance, or identity.

## Our standards

Behaviour that is welcome:

- Technical criticism of code, ideas, and designs — the sharper the better,
  aimed at the work and not the person
- Asking "why" about concurrency decisions; this codebase depends on it
- Explaining patiently; everyone was new to memory ordering once

Behaviour that is not welcome:

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissive comments
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Language that excludes people from participating

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere a participant is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, hide, or delete contributions that violate these
standards, and may block accounts that are abusive.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting channel or contact a
maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers who do not follow this code of conduct in good faith may be removed
from the project.
